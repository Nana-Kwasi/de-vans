# Rider Agreement

This agreement sets the rules for using the Qooka Qooks rider portal to deliver orders for Qooka Qooks Restaurant ("we", "us"). It covers your use of the platform and does not by itself change any separate employment or service arrangement you have with us.

## 1. Your account

- Your account is created by the restaurant. Change the temporary password the first time you sign in.
- **Keep your password secret and never let anyone else use your account.** You are responsible for deliveries taken on your account.
- We may deactivate your account at any time, for example if you stop delivering for us or break this agreement. Deactivation signs you out immediately.

## 2. Taking deliveries

- When you are online you may receive delivery requests. Accept only deliveries you can complete promptly.
- Each delivery goes to the first rider who accepts it. If you cannot complete a delivery you accepted, release it **before** you pick up the food so another rider can take it.
- After pickup, deliver the order directly to the customer without unnecessary stops.

## 3. Handling food

- Check the order against the item list before you leave the restaurant.
- Keep food sealed, upright and protected from heat, rain and dirt.
- Never open, tamper with or consume a customer's order.

## 4. Money

- **Customers pay the restaurant in the app. Never ask a customer for money for food, and never accept cash or a mobile-money payment for an order.**
- You earn the delivery fee shown on each request. Earnings are recorded in your Earnings page.

## 5. Completing a delivery

- Hand the food to the customer, then ask for their **4-digit delivery code** and enter it to complete the delivery. Never mark a delivery complete without giving the customer the food.
- If you cannot reach the customer or the code is refused, call the customer and the restaurant before leaving.

## 6. Location sharing

While you are online and during deliveries, your location is shared as described in the Rider Privacy Notice. Keep the rider page open during deliveries so the customer can follow you. Do not interfere with or fake your location.

## 7. Safety and conduct

- Obey traffic laws, wear a helmet when riding a motorbike, and never use your phone while moving.
- Be polite and respectful to customers and staff. Do not keep or use customer details (such as phone numbers) for anything other than the delivery.

## 8. Changes

We may update this agreement. We will publish a new version and ask you to accept it before you take more deliveries.
