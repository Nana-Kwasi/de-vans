# Rider Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal data Qooka Qooks Restaurant ("we", "us") collects about you as a delivery rider using the Qooka Qooks rider portal, why, who sees it, and your rights under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). We are the data controller for this data.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account details** — your name, phone number (used to sign in), email if given, vehicle type and plate number, and notes the restaurant adds when setting up your account (for example ID or emergency contact details you provide).

**Sign-in and security** — your password (stored only in scrambled, unreadable form), sign-in attempts, and the IP address and device type used when you sign in or accept these documents.

**Location** — your device's GPS location:

- **only while you are online and the rider page is open**, and
- **during deliveries you have accepted**, when we also keep a trail of points along the route.

**We do not track your location when you are offline or the rider page is closed.**

**Delivery records** — which deliveries you accepted, picked up, arrived at and completed, with times, delivery-code attempts, and the delivery fees you earned.

## 2. Why we use it

- To offer you delivery requests, assign deliveries and pay you your delivery fees.
- To show customers where their food is while you are delivering their order.
- To guide you to the restaurant and the customer.
- To prove a delivery was completed and to resolve complaints or disputes.
- To keep accounts secure and prevent fraud.
- To meet our legal and accounting duties.

## 3. Who sees it

- **The customer you are delivering to** sees your name, phone number, vehicle type, plate number and your live location — **only while you are carrying their order**. They stop seeing your location once the order is delivered.
- **Restaurant admins** see your account details, online status, last known location and delivery history, to manage deliveries.
- **Service providers** that run the service for us: Google Cloud and Firebase (hosting), Supabase (database), and map providers (Google Maps, OpenStreetMap) to display locations.
- **Authorities**, where Ghanaian law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data. Some providers store data outside Ghana (for example in the European Union and the United Kingdom) under strong security safeguards.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Account details: while you work with us and up to 2 years after your account is closed.
- Delivery records and earnings: for as long as Ghanaian tax and accounting law requires, generally up to 6 years.
- Location trails: up to 12 months after the delivery, then automatically deleted.
- Records of your acceptance of these documents: as long as your account exists and 6 years after.

## 5. Your rights

You may ask us to show you the data we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, or delete data we no longer need to keep. You may object to processing that causes you unwarranted damage or distress. Ask the restaurant admin or email us; we will reply promptly and within the time the law allows. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

## 6. Changes

When we change this notice we publish a new version. You will be asked to read and accept it before you can take more deliveries.
