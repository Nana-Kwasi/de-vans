# Customer Privacy Policy

Qooka Qooks Restaurant ("Qooka Qooks", "we", "us") runs the Qooka Qooks app and website at qookaqooks.com. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you order from us, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). We are the data controller for this data.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account details** — your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.

**Order details** — what you order, notes for the kitchen, your delivery address, the time you want it, the contact phone for the order, totals and delivery fees, and the status history of each order.

**Location** — when you choose delivery, we use your device's GPS (only while the app is open) to find your delivery point, show your delivery area and calculate the delivery fee. You can move the pin yourself. We keep the delivery point and its accuracy with the order so your rider can find you.

**Payments** — payments are handled by Paystack. We receive a payment reference, the amount, the method (for example mobile money) and whether it succeeded. We never see or store your mobile-money PIN or card details.

**Reviews and support** — ratings, comments and messages you send us.

**Device and security data** — a notification token (if you allow notifications), and the IP address and device type recorded when you sign in, place orders or accept these policies, so we can protect your account and keep proof of your consent.

## 2. Why we use it

- To take, prepare, deliver and track your order, and to contact you about it.
- To take payment and handle refunds.
- To show your rider where to deliver and to let you track your rider.
- To answer support requests and improve our food and service.
- To keep the service secure and prevent fraud.
- To meet our legal, tax and accounting duties.

We only send you marketing messages if you agree to them, and you can opt out at any time.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **Your rider** — your name, contact phone, delivery address, delivery pin and order items, only for the order they are delivering, and only after they accept it. Riders never receive your payment details.
- **Service providers who run the service for us**: Paystack (payments), Supabase (sign-in and database), Google Cloud and Firebase (hosting), Google Mail (order emails), and map providers (Apple Maps, Google Maps, OpenStreetMap) to display locations.
- **Authorities**, where Ghanaian law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Some of these providers store data outside Ghana (for example in the European Union and the United Kingdom). When that happens we rely on providers that apply strong security and data-protection safeguards.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Account details: while your account is open.
- Orders, payments and receipts: for as long as Ghanaian tax and accounting law requires, generally up to 6 years.
- Delivery locations and rider tracking: up to 12 months after delivery, then automatically deleted.
- Support messages and reviews: as long as needed to resolve your request and improve our service.
- Records of your policy acceptances: for as long as your account exists and for 6 years after, as proof of consent.

## 5. Your rights

Under Act 843 you may ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
- delete your account and data we no longer need to keep;
- stop using your data for direct marketing;
- stop processing that causes you unwarranted damage or distress.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will reply promptly and within the time the law allows. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

## 6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are never stored in readable form. Access to customer data is limited to staff who need it, and admin accounts are protected against password guessing.

## 7. Children

Our service is not intended for children under 18. Please do not create an account for a child.

## 8. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we publish a new version with a new version number. You will be asked to read and accept the new version before your next order.
