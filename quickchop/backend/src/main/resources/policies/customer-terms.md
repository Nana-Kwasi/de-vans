These terms apply when you use the Qooka Qooks app or website to order food from Qooka Qooks Restaurant ("we", "us"). By creating an account or placing an order you agree to them.

## 1. Your account

You must be at least 18 to create an account. Keep your sign-in details private; you are responsible for orders placed from your account. Give us accurate contact details so we can reach you about your order.

## 2. Ordering

- Menu items, prices and availability are shown in the app and can change at any time. The price you pay is the one shown when you place the order.
- Delivery is available within our delivery area (shown in the app). Outside it, choose self-pickup.
- Your order is only confirmed once your payment succeeds. We will email you a confirmation.
- Tell us about allergies or dietary needs in the kitchen note. We do our best but cannot guarantee any dish is free from allergens.

## 3. Prices and delivery fees

- Prices are in Ghana cedis (GHS).
- The delivery fee depends on the distance from our kitchen to your delivery pin and is shown at checkout before you pay. Orders above a set amount may qualify for free delivery when this is offered.
- Make sure your delivery pin is accurate. If you move the pin after paying, the fee does not change, but deliveries outside our area may be cancelled.

## 4. Payment

Payment is made in the app through Paystack (for example mobile money) before we start preparing your food. **Riders do not collect money for food.** Never give a rider cash or a payment PIN for an order you have already paid for.

## 5. Delivery and your delivery code

- You can follow your rider on the map once they accept your order.
- When your order is on its way you receive a 4-digit delivery code. **Only give the code to the rider once you have your food.** Giving the code confirms you received the order.
- Please be reachable on your contact phone. If the rider cannot reach you or find you after reasonable attempts, the order may be returned to us and may not be refundable.

## 6. Cancellations and refunds

- Food is prepared fresh for you, so once we start preparing an order it cannot normally be cancelled.
- If we cancel your order, or an item you paid for is unavailable, we will tell you and refund the amount within 7 days, as required by the Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772).
- If your order is wrong, missing items or arrives in poor condition, contact us within 24 hours through Help & Support with your order number and, if possible, a photo. We will put it right with a replacement, credit or refund.

## 7. Reviews and conduct

Reviews must be honest and must not contain abusive or offensive language. Treat our staff and riders with respect. We may suspend accounts used for fraud, abuse or false complaints.
