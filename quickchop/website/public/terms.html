Terms of Service

Last updated 24 September 2026

These terms apply when you order from Qooka Qooks Restaurant through our mobile app. By placing an order you accept them.

Ordering

You need an account with a working phone number and email address, since that is how we reach you about your order. Keep your sign-in details to yourself — orders placed from your account are treated as yours.

An order is accepted once payment is confirmed. We may decline an order if an item has run out, the address is outside our delivery area, or we cannot reach you to confirm details. If we decline after payment, you get a full refund.

Prices and fees

All prices are in Ghana Cedis (GHS) and include applicable taxes. Menu prices can change, but the price you see when you place an order is the price you pay.

How delivery fees work

The fee is based on the distance between our kitchen at Anyaa Last Stop and your delivery address:

ComponentAmount
Base fee, covering the first 3 kmGHS 10.00

The fee is always shown before you confirm and pay. Self-pickup orders have no delivery fee. These rates may change; the rate shown at checkout is the one that applies. We may run free-delivery offers from time to time.

Payment

We accept mobile money — MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AirtelTigo Money — processed by Paystack. Payment details are entered with Paystack and never reach us. Orders are prepared once payment is confirmed.

Delivery and pickup

We deliver within 25 km of our kitchen. Delivery times depend on distance, traffic and how busy the kitchen is, and any time we quote is an estimate rather than a guarantee.

Please be reachable on the number on your order. If our rider cannot reach you or find the address, they will wait a reasonable time before leaving. Food is not remade free in that case, because it has already been cooked and travelled.

For self-pickup, collect from the kitchen at the time agreed.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Before we start cooking — cancel for a full refund. Contact us as soon as you can.
  • Once preparation has started — we usually cannot cancel, since the food has been made for you.
  • Something wrong with your order — wrong items, missing items, or food that arrives in poor condition. Tell us within 24 hours with a photo where possible, and we will replace it or refund it.

Refunds go back to the mobile money account that paid, and typically take a few business days depending on your provider.

Food, allergies and safety

Our food is cooked in a kitchen that handles many ingredients, including common allergens such as peanuts, fish, shellfish, eggs, dairy and gluten. We cannot guarantee any dish is free of a given allergen. If you have an allergy, tell us in the kitchen note before ordering and speak to us directly if it is serious.

Please eat your food promptly. We cannot be responsible for food left unrefrigerated after delivery.

Using the app properly

Do not place orders you do not intend to pay for or receive, interfere with the app or our servers, or use someone else's payment details. We may suspend an account being used this way.

Our responsibility

We take care preparing and delivering your food. Beyond that, our liability for any order is limited to what you paid for it. Nothing here limits rights you have under Ghanaian consumer law.
