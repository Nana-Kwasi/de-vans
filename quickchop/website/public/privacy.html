Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

This policy covers the Qooka Qooks Restaurant mobile app and this website, both operated by Qooka Qooks Restaurant, Anyaa Last Stop, Accra, Ghana. It describes what we collect, why, and who else handles it.

What we collect

Account details

When you create an account we store your name, email address and phone number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google — we never see your Google password.

Order and delivery information

For each order we store the items, your delivery address, any note you leave for the kitchen or about delivery timing, the amounts charged, and the order's status history.

About location: if you let the app use your location, the coordinates are sent to our server only to calculate the delivery fee for that order. They are used for that calculation and are not stored against your account or your order. The delivery address you type is stored, because we need it to deliver.

Payments

Mobile money payments are handled by Paystack. Your mobile money number, PIN and any other payment credentials are entered with Paystack and never reach our systems. We store only the transaction reference, amount, and whether it succeeded.

Support and reviews

If you contact support from the app we store your message together with your name, email, phone number and the order it concerns. Reviews you leave are stored with your order.

Notifications

If you allow push notifications, the app stores a device token so we can tell you when your order status changes. You can withdraw this at any time in your phone's settings.

Why we collect it

  • To take and deliver your order — your name, phone, address and order details.
  • To calculate the delivery fee — the distance between our kitchen and your delivery location.
  • To take payment — passed to Paystack to process the transaction.
  • To keep you informed — order status by email and, if allowed, push notification.
  • To answer support requests — the message and order you tell us about.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept while your account is open, and afterwards only as long as needed for accounting and tax records. Support messages are kept while the issue is open and for a reasonable period after, so we can pick up the history if you come back to us.

Your choices

  • See or correct your details — from your profile in the app, or by emailing us.
  • Delete your account — email qookacooks@gmail.com and we will delete your account and personal details. Order records needed for tax and accounting are kept, with your personal details removed where the law allows.
  • Stop notifications — turn off push in your phone settings. Emails about an order you placed are part of the service and are not marketing.
  • Stop sharing location — turn off location permission. You can still order by typing your address; we will use the standard delivery fee instead of a distance-based one.

Security

Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as cryptographic hashes and cannot be read back, by us or by anyone else. Access to the database is limited to the people who run the service.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
